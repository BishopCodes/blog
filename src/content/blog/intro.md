As an introduction I am a software engineer but now more specifically a solutions architect. I
decided to start a blog after putting it off for so long. I think that I along with many others in
the industry have a perspective especially with the changes introduced by AI. Ill get to that in a
different post.

To give some background I am a self taught engineer. I have read many books, articles, and code over
the years including watched an unhealthy amount of YouTube. Professionally, I have worked IT for a
little over a decade. But have been tinkering with tech and writing code since I was about 5 or 6
which my first language was [Basic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BASIC) that my moms boyfriend at the time was coding in.

I was not very good obviously, and am not at that level of natural intelligence to be anywhere close
to genius level. I am more of a typical normal person (well normal by some standards 10 years or
more ago). [Idiocracy](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0387808/) is seriously starting to become a documentary but I digress. As I grew older I learned more and possibly did some things at my schools computers/network that caused some outages (kids do stupid things).

I grew up with the movie [Hackers](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0113243/), [Swordfish](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0244244/), [The Lawnmower Man](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0104692/), and [Tron](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0084827/) just to name a few, and they fantasize the actual boring reality of typing code into a terminal. I love old music like Pink Floyd, Journey, Queen, Supertramp to name a few but also still keep up with the newer trends some are good and some not so much but that's a given with peoples tastes. I am absolutely obsessed with Deadpool which my office reflects. And I am both a Husband and Father to two beautiful girls who all mean the world to me.

I say all of this to give some insight into my background and general character. I believe in doing things the least wrong way and speak to power when no one else will. I try to help people as much as I can even to the detriment of my own personal health. Right now I am focusing on getting my Masters in Psychology which I plan to write a book in the future talking from the lens of a Software Engineer. I am still keeping up as best as I can with the changes in tech like new languages, frameworks, patterns and so on. I hope that this blog helps me to share my ideas and perspectives to others and make me a better writer. I am using [Harper](https://github.com/automattic/harper) to help me with this a bit and try as much as possible to avoid using AI as it seems to suck the soul out of the persons writing.

Anyways till next time. Take care! Bishop out!
